# Campus Talents artwork

The 43 transparent PNGs under `src/renderer/assets/talents/` were generated with
OpenAI's image-generation tool for Maestro Leeds's Campus Talents mod. They are
community contributions supplied for use and redistribution with Venus Extracurriculars,
not Venus Dev artwork. No external stock photographs or player data are included.

The folders match the six skill trees; `shared/personal-growth.png` is the header skill.
`src/renderer/views/TalentArtwork.tsx` maps each saved skill ID to its illustration.
Changing a picture does not change the skill ID, purchases, ranks or effects. Unmapped
extension skills use the generic talent mark.

The final illustrations include Shake It Off without snowflakes, Shared Wavelength as
a heart radiating waves, and Borrowed Voice as a whisper directed into the listener's ear.
Transparency is part of the files; the panel supplies the background in either theme.

See [the asset licence index](../../LICENSE-ASSETS.md) for the separation between
community contributions and the official game's assets.
