# Campus Talents

Campus Talents adds a **Skill trees** button beside Echoes, during a scene and on the between-scene screen. It uses the game's theme and scales with the stage. Enable Campus Talents in Mods. Charm, Heart, Brain, Body and Money work independently of other mods. Echoes skills require Echo, and spirit skills also require Breakthrough. The catalog contains 43 skills: six trees with 36 numerical skills and six tree abilities, plus the shared Personal Growth ability.

The ending's crush check and next opening account for the new scene's Charm and Echo affection bonuses, including eligible Anticipation. This is a preview of the saved award: it does not pay BP or consume an ability early. Bonus amounts and skill prices are unchanged. A crush outcome already banked in an older mid-ending save is preserved rather than rerolled.

Silver Lining and Leave Them Wanting More are available once a reply finishes, including while its final line remains on screen and the game's reply box is available. Their controls explain when you need to finish reading, wait for generation, or enter a suitable social scene. Savings controls appear only under Rainy Day Fund and Nest Egg.

Each skill has its own illustration on the card and in its details. Personal Growth also has an icon in the header. Names, rank markers and prices remain visible; the pictures do not indicate a different rank or effect. This artwork update preserves the previous balance, purchases and saved progress.

For a harder fresh playthrough, enable the independent [Starting Challenge](starting-challenge.md)
mod in **Main Menu → Mods → Starting Challenge**. Then start New Game or Quickstart. The
normal −/+ rows let you choose Brain, Body and Heart independently, including **−30, −60,
−80 and −100**. The arrow rows show rank titles without numbers. The two new stat
names are **Life’s Punchline (−80)** and **Peaked in the Womb (−100)**. Untouched rows remain at zero. This option is not inside
Skill trees, and existing saves keep their stats.

## Bond Points

Each playthrough gets one initial grant equal to the sum of its positive current affection scores, rounded down. Negative relationships do not subtract from this grant. After that, newly settled liked memories have a base reward of **1 BP** and loved memories **2 BP**, scaled by the average of your Brain, Body and Heart before the scene's gains are applied. The scale changes smoothly at every value, not only the starting tiers.

| Average stat | BP rate | Liked | Loved |
|---|---|---|---|
| −100 | 300% | 3 | 6 |
| −80 | 280% | 2.8 | 5.6 |
| −60 | 260% | 2.6 | 5.2 |
| −30 | 150% | 1.5 | 3 |
| −15 | 125% | 1.25 | 2.5 |
| 0 | 100% | 1 | 2 |
| +50 | 75% | 0.75 | 1.5 |
| +100 | 50% | 0.5 | 1 |

The rate interpolates between the anchors above and is capped at 300% and floored at 50%. Mixed stats use their average: Brain −60, Body 0 and Heart 0 average −20 and give about 133.33% rewards (1.33 BP liked, 2.67 loved). Fractional BP accumulates until it makes a spendable whole point and survives saves, purchases, mod toggles and semester carry. The menu shows whole spendable BP beside a circular progress wheel, with no decimal remainder or fractional reward breakdown. The wheel fills clockwise toward the next point; completed points enter the wallet and the wheel displays any leftover progress. Spending BP preserves that progress. The table above documents the underlying balance only. Existing balances, initial grants and owned ranks are not recalculated. New initial grants still use current affection without this multiplier.

Scene, text and gift memories are pooled across characters and compared against the start of the time slot. Duplicate memories and already-settled time slots do not pay again. Negative reactions neither award nor remove BP. There is no new group-reward cap in this version.

Buying skills spends BP, never affection. Charm and Echo bonuses do not multiply BP. The initial grant happens once per save timeline; loading an earlier save returns to that save's progress, including its currency and purchases. Scene replays and the scene creator cannot earn or spend points. Updating an existing Campus Talents save adds the new trees without reseeding BP or resetting purchases.

Only new BP earnings use this curve. Ordinary skill costs, full stat bonuses and native semester decay remain unchanged. Special ability costs are Eureka 60, Leave Them Wanting More 70, Silver Lining 70, Dancer 15/20/25, Eye for Value 60 and Borrowed Voice 80 BP. Existing purchases receive no retroactive refund. After decay lowers average stats, the curve raises future earnings naturally: average 48 pays 1.52 BP per loved memory; average 15 pays 1.85. There is no separate break grant. Personal Growth retains its existing prices and weekly limits.

## Personal Growth

Open **Skill trees → Personal Growth** in the header. It is shared across the stat trees and does not replace their capstones. Fully upgrade any one second-row Body, Brain or Heart skill, then pay **60 BP** to learn it.

- Choose Brain, Body or Heart. A purchase normally adds **one actual point**, without using an activity slot or AI request.
- **New progress:** 25 BP per point below 60; 40 BP from 60 onward.
- **Recovery:** 10 BP per point to restore a loss recorded at a semester break with this version installed. Starting with negative stats or taking an ordinary penalty does not create a recovery allowance.
- **Six purchases total per calendar week**, shared across all three stats, with **at most two for new progress**. Six recovery, five recovery plus one new, or four recovery plus two new are all allowed. With no break losses remaining, only two purchases are available. Unused purchases do not accumulate.
- Purchases stop at **Godly (100)**. Normal gameplay can retain or earn stats above 100; this skill never reduces them. A fractional final point is priced proportionally, rounded up to a whole BP, and counts as one purchase.
- Semester decay is unchanged and applies to purchased stats too. Each new break records its actual losses, replacing the previous allowance. Ordinary training also uses up the loss remaining to restore; later penalties cannot refill it.
- Older saves can buy new progress immediately after unlocking. They get recovery prices after their next recorded break; earlier losses are not guessed.
- Purchase at a safe save point outside active classes, exams, project work, shifts or endings. Replays and the scene creator cannot buy points. BP, stats, recovery and weekly counts are saved together; a failed save spends nothing.

For example, Brain falling from 100 to 60 creates 40 points eligible for recovery pricing. You can buy six back for 60 BP that week, or four for 40 BP and spend your other two purchases on new progress elsewhere. You still need enough BP for every purchase.

## Activity and skill gains

Activities give their native Brain, Body and Heart rewards plus the full listed skill bonuses. A solo activity that earns 2 Brain gives 2; with Deep Focus II it gives 4. If an eligible solo scene trains several stats, each receives its full eligible reward. Crossing zero does not change these rules. Illness and other native eligibility restrictions still apply.

Average stats scale **BP rewards only**. Skill costs and purchased effects, native grading and semester decay keep their existing rules. BP scaling alone does not guarantee that all three stats take multiple semesters to reach Godly: normal practice can still raise stats quickly.

Existing save totals, BP and ranks are not rewritten. Old fractional stat records are retained as inactive compatibility data; they neither reduce nor increase new awards.

## Charm

The earlier Heart affection skills now appear under Charm, including Leave Them Wanting More. Existing ranks, prices, saved affection bonuses, Anticipation and cooldowns remain intact. The new Heart tree has its own purchases and improves the player's Heart stat.

Costs below are paid separately for each rank. The second skill in each chain requires rank one of the first. Each tree has one centered ability beneath the three chains. Fully upgrade any one second-row skill in that tree to unlock its purchase.

| Chain | Skill | Rank costs (BP) | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connection | Warm Welcome | 15 / 30 / 45 | New liked memories give +5 / 10 / 15% affection. |
| Connection | Lasting Impression | 25 / 40 / 55 | New loved memories give +10 / 20 / 30% affection. |
| Together | Group Therapy | 20 / 35 | Another +5 / 10% on positive scene memories for NPCs in a scene with at least two NPCs. Texts and gifts do not get this group bonus. |
| Together | A Little Grace | 25 / 40 | New disliked memories cost 10 / 20% less affection. Hated reactions and separate jealousy memories keep their penalties. |
| Thoughtfulness | Keep in Touch | 15 / 30 | Another +5 / 10% on new positive text memories. |
| Thoughtfulness | Thoughtful Gifts | 25 / 40 | Another +10 / 20% on new positive gift memories. |

Ordinary Charm bonuses add together, capped at **+50%** per new memory; Anticipation can add another 25% to one positive scene memory. Any Echo affinity multiplies separately. For example, a loved gift with Lasting Impression III and Thoughtful Gifts II earns 1.50× its usual affection before Echo affinity and native aging. The bonus is stamped at settlement: buying ranks later never rewrites old memories. It pauses while Campus Talents is disabled and resumes when re-enabled. This does not force a favorable reaction, romance, or forgiveness.

## Heart

| Chain | Skill | Rank costs (BP) | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confidence | Quiet Confidence | 20 / 40 | Solo activities already earning Heart give +1 / 2 Heart; excludes classes and shifts. |
| Confidence | Social Ease | 25 / 40 | Activities with company already earning Heart give +1 / 2 Heart; excludes classes and shifts. |
| Practice | Class Act | 20 | Attending a class that awards Heart gives +1 Heart. |
| Practice | People Person | 30 | Completed shifts already awarding Heart give +1 Heart. |
| Growth | Show Up | 20 / 35 | Attend an accepted invitation or agreed plan that ends positively to earn +1 / 2 Heart. Once per game day. |
| Growth | Finding Your Feet | 40 | One rank. While Heart is below zero, an activity, class or shift already earning Heart gives another +1 Heart. Stacks with the applicable training bonus; grades alone do not qualify. |

Only the applicable activity bonus pays. No Heart perk gives extra points while sick. Grade confidence is calculated separately from activity gains, so receiving a grade does not count as solo practice or social training. Finding Your Feet checks Heart before this slot's gains and stops applying at zero or higher. These are extra stat points, not affection or BP.

Finding Your Feet has one rank for 40 BP. Existing rank-I owners keep their purchase; previous rank-II owners keep the single-rank skill and receive 25 BP back. Learning this one rank satisfies its Silver Lining prerequisite.

Show Up counts accepted immediate hangouts and attended calendar plans. At least one person you agreed to meet must receive a new liked or loved scene memory; a negative scene memory from any of those invitees disqualifies the meeting. Texts, gifts, chance encounters and simply accepting an invitation do not count. The reward arrives at scene completion, at most once across day and night together, and the limit survives saving and loading. It does not trigger extra training or recovery bonuses. Replay, scene creator, class, shift and project scenes do not qualify. It uses the normal scene bookkeeping result before the optional memory editor. Existing Show Up purchases retain their ranks and prices.

## Brain

| Chain | Skill | Rank costs (BP) | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Study | Deep Focus | 20 / 40 | Solo scenes already earning Brain give +1 / 2 Brain; excludes classes and shifts. |
| Study | Active Listening | 30 | Attending a class that awards Brain gives +1 Brain. |
| Projects | Work Smarter | 20 / 35 | New project sessions count as 1.25 / 1.5 sessions of progress. |
| Projects | Late-Night Cram | 40 | Nighttime project sessions count as 2 sessions, replacing Work Smarter at night. |
| Assessment | Test Strategy | 20 / 35 / 50 | +3 / 6 / 9 percentage points on future exams attended with at least one answered question. |
| Assessment | Final Polish | 25 / 40 / 55 | +3 / 6 / 9 percentage points on future showcases attended with some project work banked. |

Extra project work takes **one normal time slot**, not a fraction of a slot. Each actual session is saved once with its credited work units; old sessions count as one. Earned extra work remains completed if the mod is later disabled. Finished work can score at most 100%; unfinished work or an imperfect exam can score at most 99%. Missing an assessment still gives zero. Recorded grades never change when skills are bought or disabled.

Starting Challenge keeps native negative-Brain grade penalties. At Brain −30 and Heart −30, a perfect exam or finished project scores 20% before perks; at −60 it scores 0%. At Brain 0 and Heart 0, perfect work scores 50%, the assessment passing threshold. Academic skills help but do not erase this debt. Training bonuses do not pay while sick and never award stats for an activity that earned none.

## Money

Costs are for each successive rank, not cumulative totals.

| Chain | Skill | Rank costs | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earn | Worth Your While | 15 / 30 / 45 | Completed shifts pay 10 / 20 / 30% more. |
| Earn | Reliable Hand | 25 / 40 | After Worth Your While I, every third consecutive shift pays an extra $25 / $50. Native strikes reset the streak. |
| Spend | Bargain Hunter | 15 / 30 / 45 | Shop purchases cost 5 / 10 / 15% less. |
| Spend | Regular Customer | 25 / 40 | After Bargain Hunter I, every third shop purchase gets an extra 5 / 10% off. Combined discounts cap at 25%. |
| Save | Rainy Day Fund | 20 | Open a savings pocket. Deposit available cash or withdraw savings through this screen. |
| Save | Nest Egg | 25 / 40 / 55 | After Rainy Day Fund, earn 1 / 2 / 3% every seven active game days, capped at $25 / $50 / $75 per period. |

Interest uses the **lowest balance held throughout the period**. A last-minute deposit does not earn a whole week's interest. Opening an empty account or upgrading Nest Egg starts a new seven-day period. A semester change, a clock rewind or a gap of more than one day between active settlements starts a fresh period; there is no offline catch-up interest. Interest stays in savings until withdrawn. Savings do not increase the game's debt limit. Shop discounts apply to catalog purchases, not arbitrary expenses described in a scene.

## Unlockable abilities

Each tree shows six numerical skills in two rows, with the three branch lines bending into one centered ability below them. All seven cards fit together. Fully upgrade **any one second-row skill** in that tree before purchasing the ability; a partial rank or a maxed first-row skill does not qualify. Select one to read its full description in the side panel; purchased abilities show their controls there. Costs buy permanent unlocks; weekly uses do not spend more BP. Calendar weeks are seven-day blocks within a semester. Dancer costs **15 / 20 / 25 BP**, **60 BP total**, excluding its prerequisite.

| Tree | Ability | Price | Prerequisite | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Charm | Leave Them Wanting More | 70 BP | Lasting Impression III, A Little Grace II, or Thoughtful Gifts II | At a fully read, open reply in a social scene, choose a present partner with a known kiss, lover or benefits milestone and say a graceful goodbye. A positive ending with no newly negative scene memories saves Anticipation and consumes one weekly use. |
| Heart | Silver Lining | 70 BP | Social Ease II, People Person I, or Finding Your Feet | Finish reading, choose someone present in a social scene and select **Turn things around**. Your next reply uses warmth, wit and humility to defuse an awkward or tense exchange and create a favorable opening. One generated reply per calendar week; a generation error keeps the use. |
| Brain | Eureka! | 60 BP | Active Listening I, Late-Night Cram I, or Final Polish III | Select an assigned, unfinished project and choose **Use on this project**. **Clear project choice** cancels it without spending the weekly use. Its next completed work session receives exactly three work units, replacing Cram and Work Smarter. Only that completion consumes the weekly use; cancellation and preparation do not. |
| Body | Dancer I–III | 15 / 20 / 25 BP | Training Partner I, Working Strength I, or Shake It Off II | Competent dancing, confidently leading a willing partner, then advanced improvisation. Dancer III also qualifies the player for Dance Instructor through Jobs, with Good Body and Good Heart, when City Life jobs and locations are present. Everyone may visit the studio. Describe dancing in ordinary input or use Start dancing. No cooldown. |
| Money | Eye for Value | 60 BP | Reliable Hand II, Regular Customer II, or Nest Egg III | In BunnyShop, select a known character to reveal her best affordable gift across all shops. Works before the first gift; uses discounted purchase prices for your budget. |
| Echoes | Borrowed Voice | 80 BP | Common Frequency II, Shared Spark II, or Measured Breath II; Echo + Breakthrough | Choose a present trusted character with an Echo and a present target. Borrow the source's existing aspect ability, at its normal discounted spirit price paid from the target's meter. Requires a player Echo and Echo abilities enabled. One completed use per calendar week; the player's own aspect never changes. |

Anticipation applies to the next later meeting with that partner. It adds 25% of base affection to one newly positive scene memory, above the ordinary Charm cap (maximum total 1.75×). It does not boost texts, gifts or BP, and expires after that meeting even if no positive memory was earned. A prepared goodbye that does not produce an eligible ending spends no weekly use. Choosing another reader action after the goodbye cancels its eligibility. Anticipation can carry across semesters; unfinished goodbye and project preparations do not.

Dancer supplies established capability to the existing scene request only when dancing is relevant. It does not force admiration, attraction or consent. Borrowed Voice reuses the existing Echo prompt and failure handling. Failed or interrupted generation returns spirit without consuming the weekly use; rewinding a completed borrowed outcome returns both. Eureka and Eye for Value are deterministic and require no AI. The goodbye's success depends on the scene actually ending positively; its numerical reward is settled by code.

Silver Lining uses the normal scene provider and its result is narrative, not a guaranteed affection award or an edit to old memories. It directs the reply toward a plausible favorable turn while retaining established consequences and character boundaries. The player chooses when an exchange needs rescuing; it does not infer hostility from a sprite or invent trouble in an already positive conversation. Any successfully generated ability reply spends the weekly use, even if the outcome is imperfect. A failed call can be retried without losing the use. Cancelling or taking a different action before a successful ability reply does not spend it. The preparation is cleared at scene settlement or semester carry; the completed weekly use is saved.

## Saves and switches

The Skill trees buttons on the scene rail and between-scene landing register through
`railButton` and `landingTool`; `gameModals` opens the existing panel under
`campus-talents`. The entry loads `TalentEntry.tsx` lazily so registering gameplay
hooks does not initialize the screen. The host supplies the button appearance,
visibility, input blocking, modal close behavior and removal when the mod is disabled.
Scene Creator and replays still omit these buttons. Opening or closing the panel
does not spend BP or write a save; purchases keep their existing queued save checks.
Game-entry and enabled-save initialization still use `ensureTalents`.

Scene stat bonuses register through `settleSceneStats`. That hook is a pure calculation used
for both ending previews and the committed reward. Show Up is added after training bonuses,
so its invitation reward cannot itself earn another training bonus. Its daily-use stamp is
committed by `slotSettled`, after the boundary's memory edits and before its save.

Previously purchased abilities and their effects remain available after this update. Any further ranks, including Dancer II and III, use the new prerequisite. Finding Your Feet receives the single-rank migration and refund described above; other ranks, BP and cooldowns are retained.

Purchases and bank transfers join the game's save queue. They are available only at a safe save point. A failed write changes neither BP, money nor purchased stats. An acknowledgement from a different loaded save cannot change the current one. Normal saves and semester carry retain the new `exTalents` data.

Switching the mod off preserves purchases, BP and savings, but stops new rewards and perks. Already earned stats, completed project work and recorded grades remain. Withdraw savings first if you want them in your wallet while the mod is off. Existing saved Charm bonuses pause, and Echo memories fall back to native Echo bonuses while Campus Talents is disabled. Switching Echo or Breakthrough off pauses only the skills that require it.

Dancing, a chosen goodbye, Anticipation and borrowed Echo abilities add short context to the existing scene request; they do not introduce a separate background AI request. Activating a scene action generates a normal scene reply and uses the configured provider. Existing prompts also receive resulting affection, stats and project progress as usual. Numerical balance is provisional and needs playtesting. Regular playthroughs have no respec or refund button.

For adding packs and custom effects, see [the framework guide](talent-packs.md) and the [example pack](../examples/talent-pack.example.ts).

## Dancer and City Life

Campus Talents has no City Life dependency. With City Life off, all three Dancer
ranks remain purchasable and keep their usual dance moves and scene capabilities.
Career guidance appears only when the playthrough includes both City Life switches.

Dancer III connects to the City Life Dance Instructor opening.
The studio is available at every rank through City Life locations; only player
hiring requires Campus Talents, Dancer III, Good Body and Good Heart. Dancer's
15/20/25 BP prices and existing scene capability are unchanged. Other talents and
other City Life jobs retain their existing dependency rules. See [City Life](city-life.md).
