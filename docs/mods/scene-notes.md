# Scene Notes

Scene Notes provides the **Shared document** button in an active scene, including a created scene. Study Together and Share Your Laptop use the same document. Enable either mod to use the library.

Choose a UTF-8 text or Markdown file up to **512 KB (512,000 bytes)**. Your configured AI provider receives the full active document with every scene request, including the closing. The panel estimates the token count. The actual count and available context depend on your model.

Replacing the file makes the new document the reference for future questions. Removing it tells the characters to stop using the upload. The scene keeps its dialogue and running summary. During laptop sharing, the screen takes priority over the document.

In a playthrough the document stays with the saved scene. A created scene holds it in memory. Documents saved by earlier previews are readable. If a saved document exceeds today's limit, the panel keeps its filename visible and pauses sharing until you replace or remove it.

## Continuing a document lesson

After attaching a document, enable **Keep studying this document until removed** to keep working through its material across replies. It is off by default. Characters still explain and roleplay naturally; when they ask a study question, they wait for your answer rather than inventing it or reading the answer key. You can ask for a hint, an explanation, or the answer.

Turning the option off explicitly cancels the ongoing document lesson and its pending question. The upload stays available for questions you choose to ask. Replacing the file—including different contents with the same filename—starts with the option off. Removing the document also cancels the lesson. Study Together respects this setting in Story mode, and the same Shared document panel works in Scene Creator. Story saves retain the document and its setting with the saved scene; Creator retains them in the active scene only.

When sharing your laptop, focusing the main game window pauses capture and pending reactions and hides the desktop character overlays. The in-game scene and cast remain in place. Starting sharing again restores the desktop overlays. Focusing the sharing controls or quick chat does not trigger the game-focus pause.
