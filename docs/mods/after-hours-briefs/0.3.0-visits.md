# Brief 0.3.0 — After Hours: night visits and the morning after

**Branch:** `mod/after-hours`. Read `docs/mods/after-hours-briefs/README.md` first and keep every rule in
it. Fetch and reset both branches from the fork, then merge `origin/hooks/after-hours`, which must be at
**20146b59** or later (Brief C, reviewed). Kit is **0.10.0**.

## What Brief C shipped (use exactly these)
- `Interlude.look?: { time?, room?: { kind: 'herRoom' | 'hisRoom'; charId } }`,
  `Interlude.onHangout?: (verdict, ctx) => { room: 'herRoom' | 'hisRoom' } | null`.
- `InterludeRow.lead?: readonly string[]` and `InterludeRow.scene?: { charId, room, action }` (exclusive).
- `bonus?: true` on the contexts of `slotSettled`, `projectSlotMemories`, `slotStatusLines`,
  `settleSceneStats`; `StatContext.bonus` for `exercisedStats`.
- During a bonus scene, the scene's `PromptState` carries `lateNight` (and its `date`/`time` are the
  night's), so `prompts.scene` tells a visit by `state.lateNight` being set.
- `playerActs` fires when a bonus scene begins and when a row's `scene` starts.
- Core keeps the visit out of the texting ledger, the roster's rolls, grades and replays, and writes the
  morning's opening again after it, keeping its picks. **The mod does none of that.**

## How the mod tells the visit began
`onHangout` returning a room only arms **Begin hangout**; the player may still end the day without
pressing it. So `onHangout` keeps the agreed `{ charId, room }` in a `modState` (`lifetime: 'game'`), and
`playerActs` commits `visit` from it only while `interludeNow()?.owner === 'after-hours'` and no `visit`
for that morning exists yet. `requests.ledger` adds its two fields only while that `visit` is set and has
no `stayed` yet.

## What the player gets
1. **At night**, any contact may invite him to her room or his, or he may ask. Agreed: **Begin hangout**,
   then a full scene in that room. It ends when they fall asleep, or earlier if it goes badly.
   **One visit a night:** once it ends, however it ends, the night is over.
2. **They spent the night together** → the morning opens on the **aftermath** in that room:
   - **her room:** a few waking-up lines, the phone open, and rows
     - **Stay with {Name}**: only if `freeForInvitation(her)` this morning. A normal morning scene with her
       in her room; the morning's narration is skipped, its texts, invitations and posts still arrive.
     - **Head back to your room**: always. One transition line, then the normal morning in his room.
   - **his room:** the waking-up lines, then one row **Start the day**: she leaves, then the normal
     morning.
   - **graduation morning:** only **Head back to your room**, then the ceremony.
3. **It ended badly** (he left, she sent him away, she left) → no aftermath and no night screen again:
   the morning opens as normal, its narration telling what happened.
4. **Busy at night:** her own asks are dropped in a chat he started (the Busy rule); his asks still arm a
   visit.

wake?: string[] } }`. `normalize` keeps `visit` only when well formed. No `carry`.

## Hooks
- **`prompts.dm`** while the night holds: the night line now allows one place, and adds a reply field
  `nightVisit: 'herRoom' | 'hisRoom' | 'none'` (which room they agreed on this exchange, `none` if
  nothing). `afterDmReply` keeps the last value per girl in a `modState` (`lifetime: 'game'`).
- **`slotInterlude`**:
  - night owed and no visit for this morning → as 0.2.0, plus `onHangout`: return `{ room }` from the
    stored `nightVisit` when it is `herRoom` / `hisRoom`, else `null`.
  - `visit.morning === to` and `visit.stayed` → the aftermath: `look: { time: to.time, room }`,
    `lines: visit.wake`, the rows above. Each row's `onPick` clears `visit`. Head back's row carries the
    transition line as `lead`; Stay's row carries `scene: { charId, room: 'herRoom', action }`.
  - otherwise `null`.
- **On Begin:** as above; `visit.morning` = the interlude's `to` slot.
- **`prompts.scene`**, while `state.lateNight` is set: "It is late at night and this visit is in {room}. The
  scene ends when they fall asleep for the night, or sooner if it goes badly."
- **`requests.ledger`**, for the bonus visit only: two fields, `stayedOver` (boolean: they spent the night
  together in that room) and `wake` (two or three short second-person lines of them waking up there
  next morning, empty when `stayedOver` is false).
- **`slotSettled`** with `bonus: true`: read `stayedOver` and `wake` into `visit`, set `nightDone` for that
  morning.
- **`requests['slot-intro']`** when a visit happened last night: one line, "Last night the reader was
  with {Name} in {room} (see the latest scene). Open on him in his own room this morning; whatever
  happened last night is behind him."
- **`hangoutVerdict`**: drop nothing for the night itself any more (only the Busy rule).
