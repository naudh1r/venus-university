# Brief 0.5.0 — After Hours: texting her while she is busy

**Branch:** `mod/after-hours`. Start once Brief E is reviewed on `hooks/after-hours`. Read
`docs/mods/after-hours-briefs/README.md` first and keep every rule in it. Fetch and reset both branches
from the fork, then merge `origin/hooks/after-hours`, which must be at **b337c2d8** or later
(Brief E, reviewed). Kit **0.13.0**. Core shipped `textWhileBusy`, `heldUntil` and `commitment` on the
texting prompt state, and `held` on `afterDmReply`, as Brief E describes; held replies show on the night
screen when they are due the morning.

## What the player gets
- A new option **Text her when she's busy** (`busy-texts`, default on).
- While a girl is in class or at work, her DM box stays open. He texts her; her reply comes in the next
  slot, written as she would send it once she is out ("sorry, had class! what's up?"). If Photo Feature is
  on, she may send a photo with it, as she decides, under Photo Feature's own rules; it comes with the
  reply.
- A late reply never starts a "meet right now" hangout; plans for another time work as usual.
- Texting her during her evening class: the reply arrives on the night screen, after class.

## Hooks
- `textWhileBusy`: `true` while the `busy-texts` option is on.
- `prompts.dm`, when the state carries `heldUntil`: one line built from her commitment:
  - class: "{Name} is in {class} right now and cannot text. She will read the reader's messages once it is
    over, in the next half of the day, and the reply you write is the one she sends then: as she would
    after class, catching up on what he said."
  - shift: the same with "at her shift at {employer}" and "after work".
  - Then: "It is not something she needs to dwell on unless she wants to."
- Nothing else: holding, stamping, hiding and Photo Feature's photo are core's.
