## Behaviour
- **Save:** `saveField<AfterHours>('after-hours', { empty: { busy: false }, normalize })`. No `carry`:
  a new semester starts empty, so Online. `normalize` reads `busy` as `true` only when it is `true`.
- **The chip** (`chatsHeader`): drawn only while the `busy-status` option is on and a game is loaded.
  Shows **Online** or **Busy**; a click switches it. Write it with the handle's `write(next, { expect })`
  when `safePoint()` is `'open'`, otherwise `set(next)` (it goes out with the next save). Hover hint below.
- **`hangoutVerdict`:** returns `null` when `busyDrops(verdict, ctx, busy)`, otherwise the verdict.
  `busyDrops` (pure, in `src/shared/afterHours.ts`):
  `busy && verdict?.initiatedBy === 'contact' && ctx.startedBy === 'reader' && !ctx.answeringInvite`.
- **`prompts.dm`:** while Busy, one line (below). Nothing while Online.
- Off (the mod or its option): no chip, no line, every verdict passes; the saved status stays.

## Text (final for 0.1.0)
- Chip: `Online` / `Busy`. Hint: `Busy: girls won't ask you to meet up in chats you start.`
- `prompts.dm` line, `{Name}` her first name:
  "The reader has set his Bunnyboard status to Busy: he would rather stay in his room for now. {Name} can
  see it, so she does not ask him to meet up right now. Making plans for another time is fine. If he asks
  to meet up right now anyway, she can react to that the way she would, such as pointing out that he said
  he was busy."
- Blurb: `A Busy status for the phone, so girls don't ask you out in chats you start.`
- offNote: `Switching it off keeps your status; girls can ask you out again.`
