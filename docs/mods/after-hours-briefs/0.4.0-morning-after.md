## What changes for the player
After a night together the morning no longer opens on narration and buttons. In order:
1. **The morning-after scene**, in the room they slept in: a normal scene with dialogue, the phone closed
   as in any scene. She knows what her morning holds and brings it up; the narration leads him on. It
   ends when one of them has to go, or once they settle into the morning together. It leaves memories and
   pays no stats or money.
2. **After it:**
   - **Her room, she is free** (`freeForInvitation`): the held landing in her room, phone open, two rows:
     **Stay with {Name}** (a normal morning scene with her, as now) and **Head back to your room**.
   - **Her room, she is not free:** no rows; the slot opens at once.
   - **His room:** no rows; the slot opens at once.
   - **Graduation morning:** no rows; the ceremony.
3. **Head back, or sent back:** the normal morning in his room. Its own opening narration (the AI call the
   game makes anyway) first tells how he gets from that morning back to his own room and into the day, in
   whatever way fits what happened; then the landing as usual, phone open. No authored transition line,
   and nothing fixed about what he does on the way.
4. A visit that ended badly: unchanged (no morning-after, the morning's narration mentions it).

## The mod's changes
- **`slotInterlude`**, for the morning after a night together (`visit.stayed`, `visit.morning === to`):
  - no `visit.scened` yet → `{ lines: [], rows: [], scene: { charId, room: visit.room, action, at: 'morning',
    pays: false } }`. Set `visit.scened = true` when it is returned (the morning's write carries it).
  - `visit.scened` and her room and she is free → the landing in her room with the two rows; each row's
    `onPick` clears `visit`. **Head back** has no `lead`.
  - otherwise → `null`, and clear `visit` (`status.set`), so the slot opens.
- **`prompts.scene`**, while that scene plays (`visit.scened` and no `lateNight`): one line built from
  `charCommitmentAt(her)`:
  - class: "It is early morning after the night {Name} and the reader spent together in {room}. {Name}
    has {class} this morning and will need to leave for it soon. The scene ends when one of them has to
    go."
  - shift: the same with "has her shift at {employer} this morning".
  - free: "… {Name} has nothing she has to be at this morning. The scene ends when one of them has to go,
    or once they settle into the morning together."
  - in his room, add "{Name} will head off to start her day."
- **`requests['slot-intro']`** for that morning, after a night together: "Last night the reader was with
  {Name} in {room}, and they woke up there together (see the latest scenes). Open by telling, in a line or
  two, how the reader leaves her and gets back to his own room and into the morning, in whatever way fits
  what happened between them; then the morning as usual." In his room, the same with her leaving his room.
  For a visit that ended badly keep the current line.
- **Remove** the `wake` ledger field and its instruction, `NightVisit.wake`, the Head back line pool,
  `morningText.leaves` and the `startDay` row. `normalize` drops an old `wake` and accepts `scened`.
- **`requests.ledger`**: only `stayedOver` remains, asked for the night visit alone (not the morning-after
  scene: skip it once `visit.scened` is set).
