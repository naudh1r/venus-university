`nightDone`) from `slotSettled` to `bonusSettled`; drop the `bonus` check, which is now implied.

## 2. The Busy line only in chats he started (finding 4)

(`threadStartedBy(conversation(charId).messages, date, time) === 'reader'`, from the kit) **and** he is
not answering her standing invitation (`state.answeringInvite` is not true: core clears the invitation as
he sends, so the thread no longer shows it). In chats she started, or
when he answers her invitation, she gets no Busy line, as the guide promises.
